how to choose

Which one is right for you?

You want speed

Photo logging wins. Snap the plate, check the estimate, adjust portions, done. That is what ProteinSnaps is built around, with protein shown first.

You want precision

Cronometer and MyNetDiary use verified databases and are best when you weigh food.

You lift seriously

MacroFactor's adaptive targets are excellent if you are happy to pay.

You are in Australia or the UK

Check the database covers your supermarket products and that energy can show in kilojoules (AU) or that portions match UK labels.

your questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free app to track protein?

It depends on your goal: for fast photo-based protein logging try ProteinSnaps; for detailed micronutrients Cronometer's free tier is strong; MyFitnessPal has the largest food database but limits gram targets to Premium.

Is there a protein tracker app without calories?

Some apps, including protein-first trackers, put protein front and centre. ProteinSnaps shows protein first while still estimating calories and macros.

Can an app count protein from a photo?

Yes. AI apps estimate the foods and portions in a photo and calculate protein. Results are estimates, so check and adjust portions.

Is MyFitnessPal free?

MyFitnessPal has a free tier with ads, but features such as barcode scanning and gram-based macro targets are on Premium.
